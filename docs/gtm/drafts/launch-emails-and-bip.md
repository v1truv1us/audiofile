## Build-in-public thread (X / LinkedIn — Launch day)

**Hook post:**
> Today I'm launching the update I built AudioFile for: shareable wishlists.
>
> I started AudioFile because I kept buying records I already owned — my collection app wouldn't load in the store. Fast collection lookup fixed the "do I own this?" problem.
>
> But the other half — *showing people what I'm hunting* — meant screenshots in text threads. So I built sharing in.
>
> Thread on what I learned 👇

**2/** The job-to-be-done: collectors have two moments — "do I own this?" (speed) and "I want this / do you have it?" (sharing). Existing tools only solve the first, badly, on mobile.

**3/** The hard part wasn't the database — Discogs owns that. It was making a collection view that loads in ~1 second on a phone, in a store, on bad signal. Lazy loading + indexing, and keeping it one-handed.

**4/** Sharing = a wishlist you can send by username. Friend gets a notification in-app, can browse it, and save public links to their own inbox. No screenshots, no "did you get my list?"

**5/** Built solo: Astro + Svelte frontend, Go backend, Supabase, Paddle billing. Premium is $5/mo for serious diggers; free tier is genuinely usable.

**6/** What surprised me: the #1 thing testers said wasn't the sharing — it was "finally, my collection loads on my phone." Speed is the hook. Sharing is why they stay.

**7/** It's live at https://audiofile.app — free, no credit card. If you collect vinyl (or know someone who does), I'd genuinely value your feedback. 🙏
