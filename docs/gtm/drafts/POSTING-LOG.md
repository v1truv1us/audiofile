## Channels & self-promo rules (quick reference)

- **r/vinyl** — strict 9:1, no direct selling in posts. Story/discussion only.
- **r/vinylcollectors** — buy/sell/trade focus. Wishlist feature relevant.
- **r/Records** — younger, looser; visual/showoff posts do well.
- **r/SideProject** — self-promo allowed; friendly. Good first launch post.
- **Steve Hoffman Forums** — no promo in Music Corner; classifieds unlock after activity.
- **Discogs forum** — low promo tolerance; enter as peer in Discogs-complaint threads.
- **Discord (Arr Vinyl / Vinyl Chat / Groove Gang)** — ask mods; use #self-promo channels only.
- **Product Hunt** — Tue–Thu, 00:01 PT; maker comment within 5 min.
- **Hacker News** — Show HN, Tue–Thu 8–11am ET; honest technical intro comment; never coordinate votes.
- **Instagram / TikTok** — anything goes; 30s demo videos perform best.
