## Verbatim quotes (by theme)

### In-store lookup / "do I own this?" (the killer use case)
> "Happens to me quite regularly including today **out record shopping**. Takes several attempts to reload it. Definitely a huge pain in the ass." — Discogs forum, *App on Android not showing entire collection*
>
> "I do wish they'd implement **offline storage** to hold details of the collection as my preferred used record store is in an arcade where **I have no mobile signal** so I'm unable to check if I have a specific album or not whilst there." — pink fish media, *Discogs site*
>
> "I can't imagine depending on a service, that could disappear at any moment, to catalog my collection. I have mine (15k items) in a **spreadsheet, I push it to my phone** every week or so, and it's always at my fingertips in any record store." — Steve Hoffman Forums

### Large collections break / slow loads
> "I have 15,000+ releases in my collection and the Discogs app on my phone invariably shows **only the last 250 additions**, which makes the search function utterly useless! ARRGGH!" — Discogs forum
>
> "Always drop to a multiple of 250... takes like **2–3 mins** and you have to stay in the app... or the sync process simply stops. It really sucks." — Discogs forum
>
> "collection is 7000+ and almost every day i have issues searching the entire collection. i get 250 releases, 1000, back to 250 etc." — Discogs forum
>
> "the mobile app **not caching** any of your collection... I have about 3000 items... takes sometimes more than a couple of minutes of waiting until I can search my collection. **Very annoying when I'm trying to remember if I already have something when at a record store.**" — Steve Hoffman Forums, *What's going on with Discogs?*

### Crashes / freezes
> "The app is basically useless right now. My Discogs freezes, the search function never finds anything and basically the app is always crashing." — Discogs forum, *App crashes repeatedly*
>
> "I have to close the app, reopen it, enter the search information over and over again — sometimes it takes me **five or six attempts** just to get information on one record!" — Discogs forum
>
> "I've always found the app crashes almost on a daily basis. No matter what phone I've used it's been doing it for years." — Discogs forum

### Grading / condition clunkiness
> "I don't like the new 'my collection' page. It's so slow that **takes ten seconds to set an item's condition rating**." — Steve Hoffman Forums

## How to use this file

- **Do** mirror the *exact situations* (in-store, large collection, "do I own this") in copy and demos — that's what resonates.
- **Do** use collectors' own words ("crate-digging," "VG vs VG+," "pressing") to signal you're one of them.
- **Don't** quote individual users verbatim in ads or name them.
- **Don't** bash Discogs relentlessly — position AudioFile as "the fast day-to-day layer," not a Discogs replacement (HN/forums respect a fair framing and Discogs's database is still loved).
- The community-manager agent appends new quotes here weekly and flags any phrase that becomes a repeating pattern (those become headline candidates).
