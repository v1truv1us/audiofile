The hero asset for launch week (Wed Aug 19, IG/TikTok/PH/HN). Two cuts: **9:16 vertical** (IG/TikTok, ~40s) and **16:9 landscape** (PH/HN/landing, ~45s). Lead angle: in-store speed pain → AudioFile demo → sharing differentiator → CTA.

## Shot list (what to capture)
1. **B-roll:** you crate-digging in a real store (15–20s, vertical) — get a friend to film or prop the phone.
2. **Screen recordings (phone, 1080p+):**
   - AudioFile: open app → search an album you own → "in collection" result.
   - AudioFile: add a new item → condition picker → photo → save.
   - AudioFile: wishlist → share → (second device/account) notification lands.
   - Optional contrast: a competitor/old flow loading slowly (blurred if naming them).
3. **End card image:** AudioFile logo + URL + "Free" (can be a static PNG).

## Voiceover
- Record clean, close-mic, conversational (not announcer-y). ~40 words total — keep it tight.
- Muted-viewer rule: the **on-screen text must tell the whole story** without VO.

## Captions (.srt) — vertical cut
Generate from the VO; burn in via CapCut or ffmpeg. Keep to 1 line, ≤7 words/screen, centered lower-third (above the IG caption area). If you want, drop the VO + this script here and I'll produce the exact `.srt` with timestamps.

## Editing checklist
- [ ] Cuts on the beat; total ≤40s (TikTok sweet spot).
- [ ] On-screen text on every beat (muted viewing).
- [ ] One ambient music bed, low under VO (-18 to -20 dB).
- [ ] Color/contrast popped; app screen legible (boost if needed).
- [ ] End card held ≥2s.
- [ ] Export 9:16 1080×1920 H.264 for social; 16:9 1920×1080 for web.

## Music
- Royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library, Uppbeat (free), or Epidemic Sound (paid). Pick lo-fi / warm — fits vinyl vibe. Avoid trending audio (copyright/region blocks).
